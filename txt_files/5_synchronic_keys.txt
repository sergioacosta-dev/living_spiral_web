the fast-paced world.

2. WORK EACH DAY TO TURN "NEGATIVES" INTO POSITIVES.
Our challenge at this time is to remain focused on the world we wish to
create. Everything serves to further. Our vibrations effect the whole.

3. 13 MOON CALENDAR.
Contemplate the energy of the daily galactic signature. This equips you with
a cosmic lens to view daily life events.

4. KEEP A SYNCHRONICITY JOURNAL.
The more you focus on synchronicities, the more they increase.

5. FORM A SYNCHRONIC STUDY GROUP.
Share synchronicities. Activate new cosmic dialogue and share your highest
dreams. Anchor a new consciousness on Earth.
